**Station 1 - Prep - Teacher1 **

Do the following:

Explain to your counselor the most likely hazards you may encounter while participating in geocaching activities and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards.

Hazard 1- How to Anticipate & Prevent? How to Mitigate & Respond?

Repeat for 5 total hazards.

Discuss first aid and prevention for the types of injuries or illnesses that could occur while participating in geocaching activities, including cuts, scrapes, snakebite, insect stings, tick bites, exposure to poisonous plants, heat and cold reactions \(sunburn, heatstroke, heat exhaustion, hypothermia\), and dehydration. TALK ABOUT:

Cuts

Scrapes

Snakebite

Insect stings

Tick bites

Exposure to poisonous plants

Sunburn

Heatstroke

Heat exhaustion

Hypothermia

Dehydration

Discuss how to properly plan an activity that uses GPS, including using the buddy system, sharing your plan with others, and considering the weather, route, and proper attire.

Discuss with the Scouts:

Why you should never bury a cache.

How to use proper geocaching etiquette when hiding or seeking a cache, and how to properly hide, post, maintain, and dismantle a geocache.

The principles of Leave No Trace as they apply to geocaching.

Talk about what you should bring geocaching and how to be prepared.



**Station 2 - Gear & Tech - ****Teacher2**

Explain the following terms used in geocaching: waypoint:, log, cache, accuracy, difficulty and terrain ratings, attributes, trackable.

Waypoint

Log

Cache

Accuracy

Difficulty ratings

Terrain ratings

Attributes

Trackable

Let them choose five additional terms to explain.

Muggles: Non-geocachers, Swag: Items left for trading, GZ \(Ground Zero\): The spot coordinates lead you to., TFTC: "Thanks for the cache.", Micro/Nano: Very small cache sizes.

Explain how the Global Positioning System \(GPS\) works.

Then, using Scouting?s Teaching EDGE, demonstrate the use of a GPS unit to the scouts. Include marking and editing a waypoint, changing field functions, and changing the coordinate system in the unit. Have them explain it back to you to make sure they understand.

Have the scouts do the following:

Explain the similarities and differences between GPS navigation and standard map reading skills and describe the benefits of each.

Demonstrate how to use the [www.geocaching.com](http://www.geocaching.com) website.



**Station 3 - Hunt - ****Teacher3**

Have them describe the four steps to finding your first cache.

Prep for homework:

Have each scout go to [www.geocaching.com](http://www.geocaching.com). Have each scout locate 3 caches close to them \(close to their house\). Have each scout write down the posted information about three of those geocaches.

Have each scout pick one of their 3 caches. Their homework is to find that cache. Make sure they know how to prove they found it so it can be signed off next meeting

Alongside that homework, give them the homework paper.

Go out and find the dummy geocache. Use what3words app to simulate the real geocaching app. If they find it, move on to a backup cache.
