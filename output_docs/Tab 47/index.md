**The "Why":** Tell the PLC that the older scouts \(Buffalos/Crabs\) need the E-Prep for Eagle, but the younger kids need the hands-on First Aid more. This plan gives everyone exactly what they need.

**The New Scout Factor:** Explain that keeping the New Scouts separate for _one_ block prevents them from feeling "lost in the sauce" during the E-Prep discussion, but merging them later keeps the Troop unified.
